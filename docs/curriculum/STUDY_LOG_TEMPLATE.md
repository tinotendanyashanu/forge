# Study and evaluation log template

Use a private per-learner copy for real activity. Do not commit names, response text or identifiable learner histories to the public repository. This blank template is not an observed outcome.

- Pseudonymous learner record used consistently (not proof of a unique person):

- Source commit / item or lesson version and artifact hash:
- Human reviewer and agreed review scope:

For each study, attempt or review, record:

- Date/time; activity purpose (baseline, study, practice, delayed check, transfer); actual elapsed time since prior study/attempt.
- Skill/subskill, lesson slug and question slug or manually assigned task identifier; existing session/item identifier where available.
- First known exposure, repeat, or unknown; earlier assessment/interview/lesson/external exposure; whether the task was withheld until evaluation.
- Completion marker/time if used. Leave actual reading duration unknown unless measured honestly outside the app.
- Hint use, external help, consulted references and whether the first attempt was closed-book. Unknown is an acceptable value.
- Objective correct/incorrect/pending status, or manual rubric criteria with evidence. Keep the ungraded free-text and AI advisory judgment separate.
- Feedback inspected after submission; short error/strategy note; exercise artifact location; next agreed check.
- Human review record and unresolved limitations; any proposed correction with separate approval status.

At the end, report counts and denominators for first submissions, repeats, matched post-completion attempts and qualifying delayed checks. Separate bank familiarity from unfamiliar-task transfer. Record dropouts, missing events and empty cohorts; do not replace them with zero performance or a fabricated learning gain. Keep manual rubric results outside automatic mastery evidence.
