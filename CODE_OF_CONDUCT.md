# Code of Conduct

FORGE contributors should be able to ask questions, disagree and improve the project without harassment. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and other project spaces maintained by the project owner.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Give technical feedback that helps someone take the next step.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and other people's time.
- Discuss disagreements in good faith, and accept a maintainer's decision to narrow or close a discussion.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, repeated personal attacks and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

**Report conduct concerns privately to [leo@leothetechguy.com](mailto:leo@leothetechguy.com).** Do not send conduct complaints through a security vulnerability form unless they also concern a software vulnerability.

Report concerns privately with links or other relevant context. Avoid posting personal details in public issues. Reports will be handled with care, with information shared only as needed to assess and address the situation; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

Maintainers may request a change in behavior, remove harmful content, limit participation or exclude someone from project spaces. They should consider context, seriousness and repeated behavior, and explain decisions privately where practical. If a report involves a maintainer, the reporting contact should identify an alternate reviewer when one is available.

This is a project-specific policy draft, not a claim of affiliation with an external conduct program. No reporting response time or dedicated moderation team is promised.
